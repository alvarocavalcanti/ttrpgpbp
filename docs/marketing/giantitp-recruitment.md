[Giant in the Playground](https://forums.giantitp.com/) "Finding Players

on Role by Post — a game ad, not a tool ad. Fill the `[brackets]` before
posting.

Tracked link (UTM): <https://rolebypost.com/features?utm_source=giantitp&utm_medium=forum&utm_campaign=recruitment>

## Title

```text
[Recruiting][<system>] <short hook> — play-by-post, 1 slot open
```

Example:

```text
[Recruiting][Shadowdark] The Sunken Barrow — play-by-post, 2 slots open
```

## Body

```text
**The game:** <one or two sentences pitching the campaign — tone, premise, and what the party will be doing. Players join for this, not the tool.>

**System:** <system and edition>. New and experienced players both welcome.

**Where we play:** Role by Post, a free text-first play-by-post app (no cost, no VTT). It keeps the whole game in one place — the timeline, dice, the status bar, and safety tools — so you can play from a phone or a browser. Take a look here: https://rolebypost.com/features?utm_source=giantitp&utm_medium=forum&utm_campaign=recruitment

**Format:** Play-by-post / asynchronous. Aim for roughly <one post per player per day / a few posts a week>; we go at the party's pace.

**Party:** <current party composition — classes, level, anything relevant>.

**Slots open:** <n>. I'm looking for <any specifics — a healer, a face, anybody at all>.

**How to join:** Reply here or send me a PM with a one-paragraph character concept. I'll reply with an invite link. First come, first served <or: I'll pick by <date>>.

**Session zero:** Before we start we'll set lines and veils and talk tone. The app has built-in safety tools (Lines & Veils plus an anonymous X-card), so we can flag anything that comes up during play without derailing the scene.

Questions welcome in the thread.
```

## Posting notes

- **Forum:** Giant in the Playground → *Finding Players (Recruitment)*. This is the correct subforum for a game ad; do not post it in the general RPG forums.
- **It must be a real game.** This is a recruitment ad, so the campaign has to actually run. Have the pitch, schedule, and at least one slot decided before posting — a placeholder ad that never starts burns the account's goodwill.
- **Account history:** post from an account with some GiTP history if possible; brand-new accounts recruiting cold read as spam. Comment in a thread or two first.
- **One thread per game, edited with updates** (slots full / closed) rather than bumped or reposted — GiTP, like most forums, dislikes repeated cross-posting.
- **Do not cross-post this ad verbatim** to RPG.net or elsewhere at the same time; adapt it per forum (see `rpgnet-tavern.md`).
- **Link:** use the tracked link above so GA4 can attribute sign-ups to this forum (consent-gated, so expect undercounting).
