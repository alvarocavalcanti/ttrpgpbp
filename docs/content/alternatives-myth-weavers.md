Myth-Weavers has hosted play-by-post roleplaying since 2006, and it is still busy: its home page reported more than 1,350 active games across 150 systems in November 2026, backed by free character sheets, dice, and forums. If you want a long-running forum game and a sheet builder that lives on the site, you can [look at Myth-Weavers yourself](https://www.myth-weavers.com).

Role by Post is a newer take on the same idea, built for how people play today. This page is an honest comparison, including the things Myth-Weavers does better.

<!-- shots -->

## At a glance

- **A chat-first timeline**, so a campaign reads back like a novel instead of a stack of threads.
- **Up to ten rooms per campaign**, each with its own status bar and privacy, so dice and out-of-character talk stay out of the story.
- **A character in every campaign** — its own name, portrait, and optional character sheet link.
- **Installable and free**, with push alerts you can tune to just your turn.

## Where Myth-Weavers is still the right home

Credit where it is due. These are the reasons people stay, and none of them is a small thing:

- **Character sheets that belong to you.** Sheets on Myth-Weavers come with templates, folders, sharing, download, and print, and they belong to the player rather than to one game. If the sheet is where your table does its bookkeeping, that is a real home advantage. [Their sheet guide](https://static.myth-weavers.com/help/character-sheets.html)
- **A directory with depth.** More than 1,350 active games across 150 systems, with recruitment listings you can read before you apply. If you want to join a table tonight, that depth matters.
- **Maps and wiki-like pages.** A game can use an interactive map canvas with tokens, overlays, and grid tools, plus Pages and Blogs for setting material beside the story. Maps are a supporter feature. [Their guide](https://static.myth-weavers.com/help/characters-resources-maps.html)
- **Granular access inside a game.** Game masters, assistant GMs, active players, and readers each get the access they need, and visibility can be set per forum, topic, or post, with whispers and GM notes for everything else. [Their GM guide](https://static.myth-weavers.com/help/game-masters-guide.html)

If a large public directory, site-hosted sheets, and forum boards are what you want, Myth-Weavers is built exactly for that, and this page will not talk you out of it.

## Where Role by Post differs

### The story reads like a novel, not a thread

A campaign is one flowing timeline rather than a set of boards. Posts keep their replies, reactions, and scene breaks attached to the moment they belong to, the timeline marks where you last read, and full-text search jumps you straight to any line. When the campaign ends you can **export the whole thing to Markdown** and keep it.

Myth-Weavers has full-text search too. The difference is the shape of the page: a topic there is a stack of posts to work through, while a Role by Post campaign is one conversation you read from top to bottom.

### Rooms keep side talk out of the story

Every campaign starts with **Main**, and the Game Master can add up to ten **rooms** beside it: one for out-of-character chatter, one for dice, one for rules questions, or a private room for a single player's side scene. Messages, replies, and rolls stay in the room they were posted in, each room keeps its own status bar and unread dot, and dice and NPC posts can be switched on or off per room — so the story room stays prose. [How rooms work](/help/rooms)

### A character in every campaign

One account holds a different character in each game. Each campaign gives you its own **character name**, its own **portrait**, and an optional **character sheet link**, so the rogue you play on Tuesdays and the paladin in the weekend game do not share a profile. The name and portrait replace your account identity only inside that campaign — messages, the member list, mentions, and the active-player picker all show the character the table knows. [How characters work](/help/account)

Role by Post is not a sheet platform. If you keep sheets on Myth-Weavers, paste the sheet's URL into the campaign and it stays one tap away.

### Game tools inside the conversation

Roll straight from a post: tap dice notation to roll, open an [ability check](/help/ability-checks) with the modifier pre-filled, or roll pools and success dice for systems that read each die on its own. Game Masters speak as **NPCs** with their own names and portraits, a persistent status bar holds initiative and notes, and **[safety tools](/help/safety-tools)** — lines and veils plus an anonymous X-card — sit inside each channel instead of in a separate document.

### Notes that live with the campaign

Three notebooks per campaign: **Character Notes** that stay between you and the Game Master, **Party Notes** everyone shares, and **GM Notes** only the Game Master reads. They are saved with the campaign rather than in a separate document nobody can find. [Campaign notes](/help/notes)

### It feels like a chat app

Role by Post is mobile-first and installable, so a campaign opens like a chat thread rather than a page of boards. Unread counts, a new-messages divider, and **per-channel push notifications** keep the table moving — set to everything, Game Master posts only, or just when it is your turn. An AFK status pauses your alerts when you step away.

Myth-Weavers installs as an app too, and it sends real-time, push, and email notifications with per-game and per-topic control. Role by Post's difference is the shape of the page and the alert: a campaign is one chat thread on a phone, and the push can be narrowed to just your turn whenever the table is waiting on you.

## Questions people ask

### Is Myth-Weavers free?

Yes. It is free to use, and donations are optional: a donation grants supporter status, which raises upload storage and unlocks extras such as the interactive map canvas, custom themes, and Discord and Bluesky integrations.

### Can I use my Myth-Weavers character sheet here?

There is no importer. What you can do is keep the sheet where it lives and pin its link in a Role by Post campaign, so it stays one tap away while the story runs in a chat timeline. Role by Post keeps a name, a portrait, and that link per campaign; the sheet itself stays on Myth-Weavers.

### Do I have to give up Myth-Weavers?

No. Some tables want a large public directory, site-hosted sheets, and forum boards, and Myth-Weavers does that well. Role by Post is the better fit when you want the story to read like a chat and the game's tools and alerts inside the campaign. Groups also run campaigns in both places.

### What does Role by Post cost?

Nothing. It is free to play, with no paid tier gating rooms, dice, notes, or notifications.

### Is this only for Dungeons & Dragons?

No. Games are played here across many systems, including a dedicated [Shadowdark](/game-systems/shadowdark) setup with its own character rules. The dice roller handles sum rolls, pools, and success dice, so systems that read each die on its own work too.
