---
title: Role by Post vs Myth-Weavers — A Modern Play-by-Post Alternative
---

Myth-Weavers is one of the best-known names in online tabletop gaming. It is especially loved for its **character sheets** and its play-by-post forums, and it has served the community for years. If you want a classic forum board plus a widely used sheet tool, it does that well.

Role by Post approaches play-by-post from the other direction: a **chat-first campaign app** built for asynchronous play on a phone. Here is how the two compare.

## The format

Both support asynchronous, written play. Myth-Weavers keeps the familiar **forum thread** structure, where a game is organized into posts and replies on a board. That structure is powerful for archiving long games and for browsing a game's history.

Role by Post presents the same play as a single **timeline**: one flowing story per campaign, with scene breaks, quoted replies, and reactions that stay attached to their post. The goal is a conversation that reads back like fiction, on a small screen.

## Character sheets

This is where Myth-Weavers is strongest — its sheet builder is a big part of why people use it. Role by Post is not a sheet platform. It keeps **per-channel character names, avatars, and character notes**, and lets each player pin a **character sheet URL** so their sheet stays one tap away. Each campaign gets its own **character name** (up to 20 characters), its own **portrait**, and its own **sheet link**, so one account can play a different character in every game without a second profile. If a built-in interactive sheet is essential to your game, Myth-Weavers has the deeper tool; if you already keep sheets elsewhere, a pinned link may be all you need.

## Rooms and side conversations

Play-by-post produces more than one kind of conversation: the story, the dice, the rules question, the aside. Role by Post keeps them apart with **rooms** — **Main** plus up to ten extra conversations inside the campaign — so the story timeline stays readable prose while the table talk has somewhere to go. Each room keeps its own **status bar** and its own **unread dot**, and the GM can make a room private to the players they invite or to themselves, or turn dice and NPC posts off in a room that should stay narrative. [How rooms work](/help/rooms)

## Notes that live with the campaign

Sheets are not the only notes a game accumulates. Role by Post keeps three notebooks per campaign: **Character Notes** (yours and the Game Master's), **Party Notes** (everyone's), and **GM Notes** (the GM's alone). They live with the campaign, are shared across every room, and update live, so nobody has to hunt through a forum thread or a separate document for the party's plan. [Campaign notes](/help/notes)

## Game tools and alerts

Role by Post builds the table's tools into the conversation:

- A native **[dice roller](/help/dice-rolling)** — tap notation to roll, open an [ability check](/help/ability-checks) with the modifier pre-filled, and roll pools or success dice.
- **NPCs** with names and portraits in the timeline.
- A persistent **status bar** for initiative, active players, and notes.
- **[Safety tools](/help/safety-tools)** — lines and veils plus an anonymous X-card — inside each channel.
- **Unread counts** and **per-channel push notifications**, including a "your turn" option.
- **Full-text search** and a **Markdown export** of the campaign.

## Mobile and feel

Role by Post is **mobile-first and installable**, so it behaves like a native app on a phone. Myth-Weavers is a traditional web application, which is fine on desktop but denser on mobile.

## The short version

| | Myth-Weavers | Role by Post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Style | Forum play-by-post | Chat-first timeline |
| Character sheets | Built-in, best in class | Pinned link + name, avatar, notes |
| Character per campaign | Sheet per character | Name, portrait, and sheet link per game |
| Rooms | Forum boards | Up to 10 rooms per campaign, private rooms |
| Campaign notes | Sheets and forum threads | Character, party, and GM notebooks |
| Mobile experience | Traditional web UI | Mobile-first, installable app |
| Dice | Built in | Built in, with ability checks and pools |

| Safety tools | Community norms | Built into the channel |
| Cost | Free | Free |

## The bottom line

If a built-in character sheet is the center of your game, Myth-Weavers remains a strong choice. If you want a **modern, chat-like, mobile-first** place to play asynchronously — with rooms, campaign notes, dice, NPCs, safety tools, and turn alerts built in — [Role by Post](/features) is designed for exactly that.
