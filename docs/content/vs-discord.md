---
title: Role by Post vs Discord for Play-by-Post
---

Discord is a general-purpose chat platform, and many tabletop groups already use it. It is free, familiar, and works well for a lot of things. The question is whether it is the best home for a play-by-post campaign specifically. Here is an honest comparison.

## Where Discord works well

- **Familiarity.** Most players already have it, so there is nothing new to install or learn.
- **Real-time voice and video.** If your group also plays live sessions, Discord covers that in the same place.
- **Community servers.** It is great for large communities, announcements, and voice channels beyond the game itself.

If your group mostly plays live and only dabbles in text, a general chat server may be all you need.

## Where a purpose-built app pulls ahead

Play-by-post has specific problems that a general chat platform is not designed to solve.

### The story stays readable

A generic channel is built for fast, ephemeral conversation. A play-by-post campaign needs a readable, searchable timeline: scene breaks, quoted replies, and reactions that stay attached to the post they belong to. Role by Post is built around that timeline, with full-text search and the ability to **export the whole campaign to Markdown**.

### Game tools are part of the conversation

On Discord, dice and character tools usually mean adding bots. In Role by Post the [dice roller](/help/dice-rolling) is native: tap `1d20+5` in a post to roll it, open an [ability check](/help/ability-checks) with your modifier pre-filled, or roll **pools and success dice** for systems that read each die on its own. NPCs, character names, and portraits are part of the message flow.

### Turns and pace are tracked

Asynchronous play needs to make it obvious whose turn it is. Role by Post tracks unread counts, marks new messages, and sends **push notifications** you can tune per channel — everything, Game Master posts only, or just your turn. An **AFK status** pauses your turn alerts when you step away.

### Safety tools live with the table

Role by Post includes **[lines and veils](/help/safety-tools)** and an **anonymous X-card** inside each channel, rather than relying on a separate bot or a pinned message.

### It feels like an app

Role by Post is mobile-first and installable, so a campaign opens like a native app on a phone instead of a browser tab full of channels.

## The short version

| | Discord | Role by Post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Communities, live voice/video, general chat | Asynchronous tabletop campaigns |
| Dice | Bots | Built in, with ability checks and pools |
| NPCs | Manual | Names and portraits in the timeline |
| Turn alerts | Manual @mentions | Per-channel push, including "your turn" |
| Safety tools | Separate bot | Built into the channel |
| Story record | Scrolling chat | Readable timeline, search, Markdown export |
