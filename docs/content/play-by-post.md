---
title: Play-by-Post Tabletop RPGs
---

Play-by-post is tabletop roleplaying played in writing, one post at a time. Instead of gathering around a table for a four-hour session, the group keeps a shared story going in a channel: you post when you can, the game waits, and the narrative grows between sessions. It is the oldest way to play roleplaying games at a distance, and it still fits busy adults better than almost anything else.

Role by Post is a chat-first app built for exactly that rhythm.

## Why play-by-post

- **No scheduling.** The game does not need everyone online at once. Post from the bus, a lunch break, or late at night.
- **More room to write.** Without a live microphone, players take time over their character's words and actions. The story reads back like fiction.
- **Everyone gets a turn.** Quieter players are not talked over. The timeline gives each post space.
- **It leaves a record.** A play-by-post campaign is a written chronicle you can reread, search, and export.

## How Role by Post works

Each campaign is a private channel. You join by invite link, with an optional password, and the Game Master controls who is at the table. Inside, everything is built around a readable timeline:

- Messages support **Markdown**, so you can write prose, **bold** a shout, or drop in a scene break.
- **Replies and reactions** keep side comments attached to the post they belong to.
- **Whispers** let you pass a private note to the Game Master without leaving the timeline.
- The **dice roller** is part of the conversation. Tap dice notation in a post to roll it, or open an ability check with your modifier already filled in. Pool and success rolls are supported for systems that read each die on its own.
- **NPCs** get a name and portrait, so the Game Master can speak as a character and keep that identity in the history.
- A persistent **status bar** holds initiative order, active players, and story notes at a glance.

## Built for the gaps between posts

Asynchronous play lives or dies on knowing when it is your turn. Role by Post tracks **unread counts**, marks **new messages**, and sends **push notifications** that you can tune per channel — everything, Game Master posts only, or just when it is your turn. If you need to step away, an **AFK status** tells the table and pauses your turn alerts.

Because it runs in the browser and installs like an app, a campaign travels with you across phone, tablet, and desktop. **Full-text search** finds any moment in the story, and you can **export the whole campaign to Markdown** whenever you want to keep it.

## What it is not

Role by Post is deliberately not a virtual tabletop. There are no battle maps, tactical grids, animated dice, or AI-generated content. It is the conversation — the part of tabletop roleplaying that play-by-post does best — with the tools that keep that conversation moving.

Bring any tabletop RPG. Generic play is built in, and optional Shadowdark character stats are there when they help.
