The **lobby** is the first screen after signing in. It has two tabs:

- **My Games** — the private channels you've joined. Each shows a preview of its **most recent message** (or *No messages yet*) with the sender's name, a short timestamp, and a small **GM** chip when you run it — plus a count of unread messages when there's something new to read.
- **Discover** — games whose GMs have **listed** them as looking for players.

With no channels yet, the lobby opens on Discover so you can find a table.

![The lobby with joined channels, each showing its most recent message](/help-images/lobby-with-channels.png)

## Discovering games

The **Discover** tab lists games that are open to applications. Each card shows the game's name, its game system, who runs it, how many players it has, when it was last active, and the description the GM wrote.

![The Discover tab, with the search box and a listed game](/help-images/lobby-discover.png)

Use the **Search games** box to look by name, description, or game system. Tap **Load more** at the bottom to see more.

Tap a card to read the full listing — the GM's description, what they expect from players (posting pace, tone, safety expectations), and how to apply.

## Applying to join

Listed games are **application-only** — there's no instant join, and every application is read by the GM.

1. Open the listing and tap **Apply to join**.
2. Enter your **character name** (up to 20 characters) and answer the GM's question if they asked one.
3. The GM reviews it and **approves** or **rejects** it.

While it's pending, the listing shows *Your application is waiting for the GM to review it* with a **Withdraw application** button, in case you change your mind. Once the GM decides, a notice appears at the top of the lobby: **You're in — (game)** with a button straight into the channel, or that the game declined. Either one has a **Dismiss** button, and it stays until you clear it. You'll also get a notification if you have them turned on.

If a listing doesn't look right, **Report this listing** at the bottom of it sends it to the server admins.

## Creating a channel

Tap **Create a channel** (+) to start your own game. You'll be the Game Master. There's a limit to how many active channels each player can have — when you reach it, the create button explains why.

Choose how the channel starts:

- **Private — invite link only** — the default. Only people you send the invite link to can join.
- **Listed — apply to join** — the game appears in Discover and on the public directory, with the **Description** and **What you expect** you write there, plus an optional question applicants answer. You approve or reject every application.

You can move a channel between the two later from **Channel Settings → Discovery**.

## Joining a private channel

Private channels are **invite-only**. Ask your GM for an invite link and either:

- Tap the link directly, or
- Paste it into the **Paste an invite link** box on an empty lobby and tap **Join**

Some channels also require a **password** — your GM will share it with the invite.

## Finding things

Use the **Search** box in the header to filter **My Games**. On the Discover tab, use that tab's own **Search games** box instead. On smaller screens the header search and the dark-mode toggle tuck into the app menu, so they never crowd the app's title.

## The app menu

Tap the ☰ button in the top-right to open the app menu (Profile, Archived Channels, Help, and more). Close it with the ✕ at the menu's top-right corner, by tapping anywhere outside it, or by pressing Escape. On a touchscreen you can also swipe in from the right edge of the screen to open it, and swipe back out to close it. Inside a channel, the sidebar's own ☰ button opens the player sidebar, which closes the same way (✕, tap outside, swipe, or Escape).

## Archived channels

Channels your GM archived move out of the lobby. You can still view them from the **Archived Channels** item in the app menu.
