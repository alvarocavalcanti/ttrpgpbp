## What's New

When new features ship, a **What's New** modal appears when you open the app. It lists the 5 most recent changes so you can catch up at a glance.

## Dismissing

- **Dismiss** — hides the modal until the next update (the next time the changelog changes).
- **Don't show again** — hides it forever. You can always reopen it manually from the **Change Log** menu item.

## Full changelog

Click **View full changelog** in the modal, or open the **Change Log** menu item and visit the **/changelog** page, to read the complete change history.
