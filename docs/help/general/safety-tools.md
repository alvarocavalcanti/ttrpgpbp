Safety tools help everyone at the table stay comfortable.

## Lines & Veils

- **Lines** are the hard limits the group agrees never to cross.
- **Veils** are topics that can come up, but happen "off-screen".

The GM edits Lines & Veils in **Channel Settings**. Each field supports up to 2,000 characters. Every member can view them from the **Safety Tools** item in the channel sidebar.

## Safety Tools Doc

The GM can link a shared document (e.g. a Google Doc) as the group's safety reference. It appears as **Safety Tools Doc** in the channel sidebar for all members.

## X-Card

The **X-Card** is a red card-with-X button in the message composer (open the **"+"** options in the composer). Pressing it flags the current scene to the GM **anonymously** — the GM sees an instant alert and can handle the scene outside the chat. The presser gets a private confirmation.
