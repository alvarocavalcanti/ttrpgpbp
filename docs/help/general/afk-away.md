---
title: AFK / Away
---

Set yourself **away** to let the group know you're stepping out.

## How to go away

1. Tap **Mark Away (AFK)** in the member menu — a small sheet slides up from the bottom.
2. Optionally type a message of up to 200 characters, e.g. "Away until Monday", and choose **Mark Away**. Leaving it blank goes away with no message.
3. Choose **Cancel** (or press Escape) to back out without changing your status.

## What others see

- An **AFK badge** next to your name
- A faded / grayscale avatar
- Your away message in the member list
- Your name struck through with an **(AFK)** tag in the active-player status bar

## While you're away

- **"It's your turn" push notifications are suppressed** until you're back.
