**Rooms** split a channel into separate conversations so the main story stays clean — an out-of-character room for table chatter, a room just for dice, a room for rules questions, or a private room for one player.

Every channel starts with **Main**, the original conversation. Rooms sit beside it in the room bar across the top of the channel — tap a room to switch to it. Messages, replies, and dice stay in the room you posted them in.

## Creating and managing rooms

The **GM** creates rooms from the **+ Room** chip in the room bar, or from the channel sidebar → **GM Tools → Rooms**. A channel can hold up to **10 active rooms**.

From **GM Tools → Rooms** the GM can:

- **Create** a room — name up to 40 characters ("Main" is reserved), with its features already chosen (see below)
- **Rename** a room
- **Archive** a room — it leaves the room bar but stays readable, and frees a slot for a new one
- **Restore** an archived room
- **Delete** a room and its messages
- **Choose who can see it** — Everyone, Only the GM, or Invited players
- **Turn features on or off** for that room — dice, Scenes, NPC posts, and whispers

The features are on by default. Pick them in the **New room** form and the room starts that way, or change them on a room you already have.

## Who can see a room

- **Everyone** — every member of the channel
- **Only the GM** — a private workspace for the GM alone
- **Invited players** — the GM plus the players the GM picks; everyone else never sees the room at all

A restricted room shows a **lock** in the room bar.

## What's shared, what's separate

- **Unread** is counted per room, so a dot on a room means new messages there
- **Search** covers the whole channel by default; from inside a room you can switch it to **This room** only
- **Rolls** always show the whole channel's history, with each roll tagged by the room it was made in
- Dice, Scenes, NPC posts, and whispers can be turned off per room by the GM
- **Images** stay in Main — rooms are text-first
- A private or GM-only room never shows in the lobby preview — only its members see its messages
