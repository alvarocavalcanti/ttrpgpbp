**Rooms** split a channel into separate conversations so the main story stays clean — an out-of-character room for table chatter, a room just for dice, a room for rules questions, or a private room for one player.

Every channel starts with **Main**, the original conversation. Rooms sit beside it in the room bar across the top of the channel — tap a room to switch to it. Messages, replies, and dice stay in the room you posted them in.

## Creating and managing rooms

The **GM** creates rooms from the **+ Room** chip in the room bar, or from the channel sidebar → **GM Tools → Rooms**. The room bar is there for the GM from the first visit, even before the first room exists, so **+ Room** is always one tap away. A channel can hold up to **10 active rooms**.

**+ Room** opens one dialog with everything the room needs:

- **Name** — up to 40 characters ("Main" is reserved)
- **Features** — Dice, Scenes, NPCs and whispers, all on by default
- **Who can see it** — Everyone, Only the GM, or Invited players
- **Players** — when you pick **Invited players**, a checklist of the channel's players appears; the GM is never on it, and neither is anyone blocked in the game

The room and its invited players are saved together, so if the server refuses the room nothing half-built is left behind, and the confirmation says who can see it — "Visible to 3 invited players", never "everyone" for a private room.

The **gear** beside **+ Room**, and **GM Tools → Rooms**, open the room manager. From there the GM can:

- **Rename** a room
- **Archive** a room — it leaves the room bar but stays readable, and frees a slot for a new one
- **Restore** an archived room
- **Delete** a room and its messages
- **Choose who can see it** — Everyone, Only the GM, or Invited players
- **Turn features on or off** for that room — dice, Scenes, NPC posts, and whispers

The manager does not create rooms; **+ Room** does.

## Who can see a room

- **Everyone** — every member of the channel
- **Only the GM** — a private workspace for the GM alone
- **Invited players** — the GM plus the players the GM picks; everyone else never sees the room at all

A restricted room shows a **lock** in the room bar.

## What's shared, what's separate

- **Unread** is counted per room — and for Main — so a dot on a room (or on Main) means new messages there, and opening one never clears another
- **Status** is per room too — Main and each room keep their own status bar, so an initiative order in one room never shows up in another
- **Search** covers the whole channel by default; from inside a room you can switch it to **This room** only
- **Rolls** always show the whole channel's history, with each roll tagged by the room it was made in
- Dice, Scenes, NPC posts, and whispers can be turned off per room by the GM
- **Images** stay in Main — rooms are text-first
- A private or GM-only room only shows in the lobby preview for the players who can open it
