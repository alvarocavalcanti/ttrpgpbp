The **channel sidebar** holds everything you need while in a channel. Open it with the person icon on mobile; it's always visible on larger screens.

## Available items

- **Member List** — everyone in the channel, with role badges, AFK states, and character info
- **Notifications** — configure what you're notified about for this channel
- **Safety Tools** — view the channel's Lines & Veils, or press the X-Card
- **Rolls** — the channel's dice roll history
- **Search** — find messages in the channel history
- **Settings** — GM only; configure the channel itself

## Link items

Depending on what the GM has configured, the sidebar also shows:

- **Map** — a link to the campaign map
- **Resources** — a link to shared resources (folder, PDF, etc.)
- **GM Resources** — GM-only resources link
- **Safety Tools Doc** — a link to the group's safety document
